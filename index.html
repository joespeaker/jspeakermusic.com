02 — about

music you miss

jspeaker makes indie pop rooted in nostalgia and quiet vulnerability. The songs pair soft, warm production with honest storytelling: late nights, fading relationships, and the feeling of looking back while moving forward.

Raised in small-town Nebraska and now based in Kansas City. His debut album, Letters to a Ghost, is on the way.
