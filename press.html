bio

Jspeaker makes indie pop rooted in nostalgia and quiet vulnerability. His songs pair soft, warm production with honest storytelling, capturing late nights, fading relationships, and the feeling of looking back while moving forward.

Originally from small-town Nebraska and now based in Kansas City, jspeaker released the single reruns in July 2026. His debut album, Letters to a Ghost, is coming soon.
